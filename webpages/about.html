About Me

I have long been curious about how things work and drawn to logic. Computer science videos first deepened my interest during the pandemic, and hands-on experimentation has helped me learn along the way. I chose IT because it feels like a grounded, practical way to explore how computing works. My long-term goal is to specialize in operational security (OPSEC) and defensive cybersecurity to help protect real people and organizations from real threats.

Interests

I am especially fascinated by cryptology and code-breaking. I also enjoy internet ARGs and investigating the clues and information they bring together. Networking and systems interest me as practical foundations for understanding how to protect people and organizations through OPSEC and defensive security.

My Computing Journey

My computing journey started before IT111, so the course is part of a longer path rather than my first exposure to programming. One concept from IT111 that caught my interest is cybersecurity. I am interested in how software fits into a larger computing environment: the systems it runs on, the networks that connect it, the physical infrastructure behind those networks, and the people who depend on it. Understanding those layers helps explain where risks come from and how they can be reduced. I plan to build on my coding experience by strengthening my networking and systems fundamentals before specializing further in OPSEC and defensive security.
